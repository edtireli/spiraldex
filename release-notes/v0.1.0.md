Your world, in Japanese. The first standalone **SpiralDex** preview brings a Classic Dex interface to Android, with a local Mac companion for photo recognition and Japanese vocabulary cards.

### Downloads

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **SpiralDex-0.1.0.apk** | Signed Android app; Android 9 or newer |
| **SpiralDex-Mac-Host-0.1.0.zip** | Mac companion, launcher, source, and universal Apple Vision helper; macOS 14+ |
| **SpiralDex-Demo-0.1.0.zip** | Standalone static project page and interactive sample demo |
| **SHA256SUMS** | SHA-256 checksums for the files above |

Install the APK, prepare Python 3.10+ and Ollama with `gemma3:12b` on your Mac, then extract the Mac bundle and open `Start SpiralDex.command`. Enter its host address, pairing token, and certificate fingerprint in the app. The Mac launcher installs Pillow on first use; model weights are downloaded separately.

**[Setup guide](https://github.com/edtireli/spiraldex#quick-start) · [Live demo](https://edtireli.github.io/spiraldex/) · [Verification details](https://github.com/edtireli/spiraldex/blob/v0.1.0/TESTING.md)**

### Included

- Native camera/photo picker and installed Japanese text-to-speech.
- Paired HTTPS upload to your Mac, background removal, and local model identification.
- Fixed vocabulary card templates, editable labels, gold/green frames, and interactive foil finishes.
- Local saved collections, recall practice, and 46 basic characters in each kana syllabary.
- New photographic chair, apple, and cup assets; public sample demo and walkthrough.

### Preview limits

Generated labels and language may be wrong; review every card. New scans need an awake Mac on the same network. No iOS build, cross-device sync, offline scan queue, voiced/combined kana, or spaced repetition yet. The Mac companion is not notarized. This is a direct-download Android preview, not a Play Store release.
