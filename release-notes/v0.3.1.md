# Cards that scale correctly. Swipe either way.

Archive cards now scale as a complete printed layout, avoiding Android WebView's minimum-font enlargement that previously caused clipped headings and overlapping text. A single discovery uses the available archive space; multi-card pages have readable Japanese/English labels and show two cards on short screens or four on taller screens. Full notes remain available in inspection.

The first swipe from the scanner can go **left or right**. It always advances from the leftmost dot to the middle dot (Cards). Continue in that direction for the last dot (Kana); reverse to go back. Swiping does not wrap past the last dot. D-pad and keyboard navigation remain available.

Phone tilt, the real upstream foil CSS, model-assessed rarity, saved collections, and pairing are retained.

Install `SpiralDex-0.3.1.apk` over the previous app. This UI fix does not require replacing your Mac host. Mac host, demo, corresponding source ZIP, and checksums are also included.

Verified with long-entry fixtures in Chromium and WebKit at 320–430 px, plus Android WebView. See [verification](https://github.com/edtireli/spiraldex/blob/v0.3.1/TESTING.md).
