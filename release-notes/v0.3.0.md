# Actual card effects. Model-assessed rarity.

The cards now load the real **pokemon-cards-css** styles by Simon Goellner, pinned at commit `acb1197633e749a1fba4412231db2f6581586d00`. The upstream files are bundled verbatim, with their license and a hash manifest. Gold/silver borders, trading-card proportions, illustration panels, ability/attack rows, and printed stats replace the previous app-like card layout.

The **model decides the object's rarity**, explains why, and selects a compatible finish:

- Common / Uncommon → non-holo.
- Rare → holo or reverse holo.
- Ultra rare → full-art holo.

There is no word-based rarity roll. Existing entries without a model assessment show **Unassessed** until rescanned. HP and field ratings remain collectible game attributes. Rarity judgments and object labels can be wrong.

**Tilt your phone to catch the light.** Cards gently rotate and move with the phone, driving the real upstream foil effects. Native Android motion starts automatically while cards are visible. Recenter from inspection or switch phone tilt off in settings. Reduced motion is respected; sensors pause in the background. The browser studio includes a motion-access button where permission is required. Sensor data stays on your device.
